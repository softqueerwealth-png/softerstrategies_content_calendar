## Overview

This interactive calendar supports the content strategy and digital brand management of Lissa Jean Ferrell, Esq.

The calendar provides a visual overview of upcoming content, creative storytelling concepts, proposed publishing dates, and production status.

The experience is designed to make content planning collaborative, accessible, and easy to navigate.

## Features

- Interactive monthly and weekly calendar views
- Clickable content concepts and storytelling details
- Proposed publishing dates and content statuses
- Responsive design for desktop and mobile
- Dropbox integration for Raw Clips uploads and Content Review
- Editorial design featuring Softer Strategies' mauve, cream, and chocolate palette

## Content Workflow

1. **Capture:** Lissa organically documents moments from her life.
2. **Create:** Softer Strategies develops content and storytelling concepts.
3. **Review:** Edited content and captions are reviewed through Dropbox.
4. **Publish:** Approved content is scheduled and distributed.
5. **Reiterate:** Audience engagement and client feedback inform future content.
